# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using **SQL and Power BI** to understand revenue, profitability, customer performance, product categories, order status, returns, payment methods, and regional sales performance.

The project follows a practical data analytics workflow:

**Raw Data → SQL Analysis → Data Preparation → Power BI → DAX → Interactive Dashboard → Business Insights**

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce business performance and answer key business questions such as:

- How much revenue and profit is the business generating?
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which states contribute the most revenue?
- How is revenue changing over time?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?
- What proportion of revenue comes from completed orders?
- What are the major return reasons?
- How do revenue and costs compare over time?

## Key Business Insights

### Strong Overall Profitability

The business generated **64.03M in revenue** with **22.73M in profit**, resulting in an overall **35.50% profit margin**.

### Beauty Leads Revenue

The **Beauty** category generated approximately **10.7M in revenue**, making it one of the strongest contributors to overall sales.

### Maharashtra Leads State Sales

**Maharashtra** recorded the highest revenue among the analyzed states, highlighting its importance as a key regional market.

### Completed Orders Drive Revenue

Completed orders contributed approximately **78.6% of total revenue**, showing that successful order fulfillment is the primary revenue driver.

### Returns Require Attention

Returned orders represent approximately **10% of total orders**, creating an opportunity to analyze return reasons and identify ways to improve customer satisfaction.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Investigate the products driving strong performance in the Beauty category and evaluate opportunities for inventory and promotional optimization.

2. Analyze customer and product performance in Maharashtra to understand the factors contributing to its strong revenue.

3. Monitor incomplete and unsuccessful orders to identify operational issues affecting revenue realization.

4. Analyze return reasons by product category and customer segment to identify recurring issues.

5. Track revenue, cost, and profit trends regularly to identify changes in business performance.
